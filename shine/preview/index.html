How the diamond shines

  • A fully completed day adds +5% energy. A fully missed day removes −8%. Partial days count proportionally.
  • Around 20 perfect days make it radiant, and slacking drains it faster than it builds.
  • Days with nothing scheduled change nothing. Today never costs you energy.
  • Energy sets spin speed (up to ~20× faster), opacity and the halo.
